# KindleHermes OS (v1)

A modular, interactive e-ink OS, context-aware lock-screen engine, and cross-device web companion built for the **Kindle Paperwhite 10th Gen (PW4 — `1072 × 1448` @ 300 PPI)** — while keeping your Kindle 100% normal for reading books.

## Highlights
* **1-Tap Standalone Hermes OS Environment:** Launch from your Kindle Library into a fullscreen `1072 × 1448` app launcher (`Today`, `Calendar`, `Planner`, `Pomodoro`, `Weather`, `Briefing`) and tap `EXIT` anytime to return to your books.
* **Truly Interactive & Zero-Touch Online Updates:** Built on a **Server-Driven Screen + Automatic Hitbox Protocol**. Deploy new layouts or plug-in apps online once — your Kindle immediately gets the new screens and interactive buttons without ever plugging in a USB cable.
* **Smart Context-Aware Lock Screen:** Lock your Kindle inside Hermes OS to show **`HAPPENING NOW`** + **`NEXT UP`**, **Rest of Today's Events**, **Top Tasks**, and **Current + Hourly Weather**. Outside Hermes OS, your Kindle acts 100% stock normal.
* **Direct Google Calendar OAuth2 & Auto Location/Time Sync:** Connect all your Google Calendars in `/app`, and auto-sync location & timezone from your iPhone/MacBook.
* **Cross-Device Companion PWA (`/app`) & PW4 Simulator (`/preview`).**
